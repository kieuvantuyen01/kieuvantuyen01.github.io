\section*{RESEARCH VISION}
\noindent
I am highly motivated to pursue PhD research at the intersection of \textbf{Optimization} and \textbf{Artificial Intelligence}. My strong foundation in combinatorial optimization and SAT solving positions me well to explore emerging research directions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Leveraging \textbf{Large Language Models (LLMs)} to automatically formulate, encode, and solve optimization problems from natural language descriptions.
    \item Developing LLM-assisted approaches for generating efficient SAT/MIP encodings and selecting appropriate solver configurations.
    \item Applying optimization techniques to real-world decision-making problems in \textbf{transportation systems}, \textbf{logistics}, and \textbf{resource allocation}.
