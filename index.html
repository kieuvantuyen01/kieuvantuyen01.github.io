Kieu Van Tuyen (Kiều Văn Tuyên)

I am a researcher in Computer Science at VNU University of Engineering and Technology (VNU-UET), Hanoi, Vietnam. I recently earned my Master of Science in Computer Science (December 2025) with highest honors (GPA 3.92/4.00, thesis defense score 9.8/10.0), advised by Dr. To Van Khanh at the Laboratory of Embedded Systems.

My research focuses on Boolean Satisfiability (SAT), MaxSAT, and Combinatorial Optimization. I develop novel compact constraint encoding methods (such as staircase at-most-one, sequential counter, and precedence propagation) for complex NP-hard problems in task scheduling, train rescheduling, two-dimensional packing, and assembly line balancing, frequently outperforming commercial solvers like IBM CPLEX, Gurobi, and Google OR-Tools.

I am also interested in the intersection of Large Language Models (LLMs) and mathematical optimization for automated model formulation and solver configuration.

I am actively seeking PhD opportunities in Optimization, Automated Reasoning, and Artificial Intelligence starting in 2026/2027.
